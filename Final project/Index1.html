HERStory

A Life of Service and Historic Milestones

Barack Hussein Obama II is an American attorney and politician who served as the 44th president of the United States from 2009 to 2017.

A member of the Democratic Party, he was the first African American president in U.S. history.

Before his presidency, he served as a U.S. Senator representing Illinois and as an Illinois State Senator. His landmark achievements include overseeing economic recovery after the Great Recession and signing the Affordable Care Act into law.

His message of "Hope" and "Change We Can Believe In" inspired a generation of Americans and people worldwide.
